---
title: Terminals
description: Run shells next to your chats and files, in tabs and split panes.
---

Each project in Troupe has its own terminals. A terminal opens in the project folder, so you can run `git`, your build, or your tests without a `cd` first. Terminals sit in tabs, next to chats and open files, and you can split the window to see several at once.
