title: Follow the agent's work
description: Read the agent's plan, steps, and diffs, review changed files, and queue, stop, edit, or retry your prompts.
---

While an agent works, the chat shows what it is doing step by step. You can read its plan, open each file it touched, and line up your next requests. You can also stop it at any time.

![A finished chat. The plan sits at the top with all four tasks crossed out. Below it are your message, a collapsed "Worked for 1s" row, and the agent's reply.](/screenshots/agents-chat.png)

## Read the plan

Many agents write a plan before they start. The plan stays at the top of the chat while you scroll. Its header shows **Plan** and how many tasks are done, such as **3/4**. A spinner shows while a task is in progress.

Each task has a status icon and a priority: **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**. Finished tasks are crossed out. Click a task that has details to read them. Click the header to collapse or expand the plan. On a phone, the plan starts collapsed.

## See each step

While the agent works, a **Working…** row sits above its reply. When the turn ends, the row changes to **Worked for** and the time it took, with the number of lines added and removed. Click the row to open it. If a step failed, the row is red and says "needs attention".

Inside, the steps are grouped, for example "Read 1 file, ran 1 search". Click a group to see its steps. Each step names what the agent did and what it did it to, such as **Ran** `bun test`. A file step shows the file name, such as `cart.ts`, and a read can add the lines it read. A failed step has a failure icon.

![An opened step list. A Thought row and a group of reads come first. The opened "Edited 1 file, ran 1 command" group shows a short diff of cart.ts, followed by "Ran bun test".](/screenshots/agents-tool-cards.png)

- Click a step to see its output, such as a command's output and exit code, or the file content the agent read.
- Click **Open file** on a step to open that file in the editor.
- An edit shows a short preview of its diff. Click the preview to see the whole diff. Click the file name above it to open the file at the first changed line.
- A **Thought** row holds the agent's reasoning and shows how many lines it has. Click it to read it.

## Review changed files

When the agent edited, deleted, or moved files in this chat, a status pill shows at the top right of the chat. It shows how many files changed and how many lines were added and removed, and how many subagents run or ended. Click the pill to open the list. When there are both changed files and subagents, the list has a **Changed files** tab and a **Subagents** tab. On a phone the list opens as a sheet, and you can swipe between the tabs.

The picture below was taken when the list was a bar above the message box. The list itself is the same.

![The opened Changed files list shows cart.ts in the src folder, marked M, with 3 lines added and 2 removed.](/screenshots/agents-changed-files.png)

Each file has a letter: **M** for modified, **D** for deleted, or **R** for renamed. Click a file to open it in the editor. A deleted file does not open. This list is for review only. To stage and commit the changes, use the **Changes** tab in the side panel. See [Git](/docs/git).

## Follow subagents

Some agents hand part of a task to a helper agent, called a subagent. Subagents that the agent starts together show as one group in the turn. The group header shows up to three agent icons, the number of subagents, a summary such as "1 working · 1 done · 1 failed", and how long they have run. The group stays open while a subagent runs and folds when all of them finish. A single subagent shows as one card without the header. The group stays in the chat after the turn ends, and when you reopen the chat later.

Each row shows the subagent's title and type, its latest step while it runs or the first line of its result when it ends, its status (**Running**, **Done**, **Failed** or **Stopped**) and its time.

Click a row to open the subagent. On a wide window it opens beside the chat; on a narrow one it slides in from the right, and on a phone it opens as a full-height sheet. The panel shows:

- **Task**, the prompt the agent gave the subagent. Click **Show full prompt** for the rest of a long prompt.
- **Activity**, the steps the subagent ran. Click a step to expand it. A subagent that started its own subagents shows them here too; click one to open it, and click **Back** to return.
- **Latest message** while the subagent runs, or **Result** when it ends.

Use the left and right arrow keys to move to the other subagents of the chat. When the agent did not report a section, the panel says why.

### Stop one subagent

To stop one subagent and leave the others running, click **Stop** in its panel. On a phone, tap the stop button on its row in the **Subagents** sheet and confirm. The work it did so far is kept. **Stop** shows only while the subagent runs and when the agent supports it. To stop the whole turn instead, use the Stop button of the chat.

### Subagents that run in the background

An agent can end its turn while subagents it started keep working in the background. The chat then keeps those subagents as running, and the [status pill](#review-changed-files) says "Waiting on N background subagents". While they run, Termul does not switch the agent or the Claude account of the chat, because that would stop them.

## See earlier steps of a long turn

A long turn shows only its latest 40 steps. Click **Show N earlier steps** at the top of the turn to load 50 more.

## See images, videos, and audio

When the agent's reply includes an image, a video, or an audio clip, the chat shows it in the reply. This works in the desktop app and in a browser, for a file in your project, a file on the web, or one the agent sends inside its reply.

- When the reply has two or more images in a row, they show as one row of square tiles. Scroll the row sideways to see them all.
- Click an image or a tile to open it full screen. Images that a message sends together, and the tiles in one row, open in the same viewer.
- In the viewer, swipe, use the left and right arrow buttons at the sides, or press the left and right arrow keys to move between images. The bottom of the viewer shows where you are, for example "2 of 5". A single image shows no count.
- Click the download button at the top right of the viewer to save the image. In the desktop app, a save dialog opens. In a browser, the browser downloads the file. For an image from the web that Termul cannot save, Termul asks to open the image link in your browser instead. If the download fails, Termul shows "Failed to download image".
- To close the viewer, click the **X** at the top left, click outside the image, or press `Esc`. On a phone, the back button also closes it.
- A video from your project first shows its file name. Click it to load the video, and it starts playing. A video from the web shows a player right away.
- An audio clip shows a player.
- When the agent links to an image or video file in the middle of a sentence, the link stays in the sentence, and the preview shows below that paragraph.

A video can be up to 100 MB. If a video is too large or cannot be read, Termul says why in its place, for example "clip.mp4 is too large to play inline (128.0 MB, limit 100.0 MB)". If an image cannot be shown, its name shows instead.

## Queue prompts

You do not have to wait for the agent to finish. Type your next message and press `Enter`. While the agent works, the send button becomes **Queue message**, and your message goes into a queue above the message box.

![Two queued messages above the message box while the agent runs tests. The first message shows the Send now and Remove from queue buttons.](/screenshots/agents-queue.png)

Termul sends the queued messages in order, one each time the agent finishes a turn. Click the **Queued** header to collapse the list. Hover a queued message to see its buttons. On a touch screen, the buttons are always visible. Each queued message has these buttons:

- **Send now** stops the agent's current turn and sends this message right away.
- **Remove from queue** deletes the message without sending it.

You cannot edit a queued message. Remove it and type it again.

### When the agent works after its turn

An agent can keep working after its turn ends. For example, Claude Code starts a turn of its own when a background command or a subagent finishes. While that happens, the chat shows the same working cue and the same stop button as during a turn. A message you send goes into the queue and goes out when the work ends. Termul counts the work as ended when no step is still running and the agent sends nothing for 30 seconds.

## Stop a turn

To stop the agent, do one of these:

- Press `Esc` in the message box.
- Click **Cancel turn**, the square button. It takes the place of the send button while the agent works and the message box is empty.

Termul tells the agent to stop. Any approval request or question that was waiting is cancelled. If you have queued messages, Termul sends the next one.

This also works while the agent works after its turn. Termul tells the agent to stop that work, and the chat shows it as idle right away.

## Edit or retry a message

Hover a message to see its buttons. The last message in the chat always shows them.

- **Edit**, on your message, puts its text back in the message box. Change it and send it as a new message. The earlier messages and replies stay in the chat.
- **Retry**, on a finished reply, sends your last message again as a new turn. The old reply stays above the new one. Retry is hidden while the agent works.
- **Copy** copies the message. On the agent's reply, it copies everything the agent wrote in that turn.

![Your message, "Try a discount parameter on cartTotal", with the Edit and Copy buttons below it.](/screenshots/agents-message-actions.png)

If the chat stopped, **Retry** starts the agent again before it sends your message. When a turn ends with an error, the error notice has its own **Retry** or **Resume chat** button.

Code blocks in a reply have their own **Copy** and **Download** buttons. Tables have copy, download, and **View fullscreen** buttons.

When you click a link in a reply or in a **Thought**, Termul asks first. The **Open external link?** dialog shows the full address. Click **Open** to open it, or **Cancel** to stay in the chat. Pressing `Esc` also cancels. Clicking outside the dialog does not close it. A link to a heading or footnote in the same reply, one that starts with `#`, scrolls to it without asking.
