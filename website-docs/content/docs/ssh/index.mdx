<Callout type="info">This works in the desktop app only. The browser version of Termul does not show SSH hosts. See [Features that use the server's own accounts](/docs/remote/server-options#features-that-use-the-servers-own-accounts).</Callout>

Termul can open a terminal on a remote server over SSH. SSH is the standard way to sign in to a server from your computer. Save a server once as a host, then connect to it with one click. While you are connected, you can also browse, upload, download, and edit the server's files.

## Find SSH in the app

The SSH hosts live in two places:

- **In the sidebar.** When the sidebar is expanded, the **SSH** section sits under your projects. Click the chevron next to **SSH** to collapse or expand it.
- **In the narrow rail.** When the sidebar is collapsed, click the **SSH hosts** icon to open the host list.

Click **Open SSH hosts** in the **SSH** section to open the full SSH workspace. It has a **Hosts** tab with all your saved hosts, and one tab for each open session. To go back to your projects, click a project in the sidebar. Your SSH sessions keep running in the background.
