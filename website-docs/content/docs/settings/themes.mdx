---
title: Themes
description: Pick a color theme for Troupe, in a light or a dark version.
---

A color theme sets the colors of the whole app, terminals included. Troupe has ten theme families. Each family has a dark version and a light version.

## Open the theme picker

Use one of these:

- Press `Ctrl+Alt+T` (`Cmd+Option+T` on macOS).
- Select the **Color themes** button (the palette icon) at the bottom of the sidebar. The button shows when the sidebar is expanded.
- Press `Ctrl+K` and choose **Change Color Theme**.

![The Color Themes picker next to the sidebar, with Tokyo Night previewed](/screenshots/settings-theme-picker.png)

## Pick a theme

1. Open the theme picker.
2. Move the pointer over a theme, or use the arrow keys. The app shows the theme right away as a preview.
3. Click the theme or press `Enter` to apply it.

To find a theme by name, type in **Search themes...**. To close the picker and keep your old theme, press `Esc`. A check mark shows the theme that is applied now.

## Choose light or dark

Each family is listed twice: once by its name, for the dark version, and once with **Light** after the name. To switch to a light theme, pick a row that ends in **Light**, such as **GitHub Light**.

![Troupe with the GitHub Light theme applied](/screenshots/settings-light-theme.png)

Troupe does not follow the light or dark mode of your operating system. The theme you pick stays until you pick another one.

## Theme families

The default theme is **Troupe**, dark. These are the families:

- Troupe
- Cursor
- Catppuccin
- Dracula
- Nord
- Gruvbox
- Tokyo Night
- Ayu
- One Dark
- GitHub

To change the color of one project in the sidebar instead, see [Project settings](/docs/settings/project-settings).
